Lumen Terms of Use

Last updated: October 5, 2026

These terms apply to the Lumen iOS and iPadOS app published by Eulices Lopez.

The product

Lumen is a privacy-first on-device AI assistant. The Free tier includes Apple Intelligence chat on supported hardware, voice input, conversation history and search, and one Ollama Local server that you configure. Lumen Pro is optional and unlocks memories, document import, Ollama Cloud, Home Screen widgets, and Siri/Shortcuts.

Lumen does not require an account and does not operate a backend.

Purchases

Lumen Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase system. The amounts below are US / USD reference prices. Apple's localized App Store price at checkout is the one that controls.

  • Pro Monthly: US $2.99 per month, auto-renewing.
  • Pro Annual: US $19.99 per year, auto-renewing, with a 7-day free trial for eligible Apple IDs. This is the default offer.
  • Pro Lifetime: US $39.99 one-time. A US $29.99 launch price may be offered for 30 days.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Restore Purchases re-downloads entitlements for the same Apple ID. Lumen never sees your payment details.

If a subscription expires or Apple revokes a purchase, Pro features lock again. Local data stays on the device.

Acceptable use

Use Lumen only on devices you own or control. You are responsible for any Ollama server or API key you connect. Do not use Lumen to break the law or to generate content you intend to present as professional, medical, legal, or financial advice without independent verification.

AI output

Lumen can make mistakes. Model output may be incomplete, outdated, or wrong. Verify important information before you rely on it. Lumen is not a substitute for professional judgment.

Privacy

The Privacy Policy describes what stays on device and what happens if you enable Ollama Cloud.

Availability

Apple Intelligence requires supported hardware and an enabled Apple Intelligence setting. On devices that cannot run it, chat requires an Ollama Local server you provide. That is a device limit, not a defect.
